## Who Josh is

Joshua Agarwal is a professional engineer (P.Eng) and MBA candidate at the Wharton School (Class of 2028), majoring in Artificial Intelligence for Business and Finance. He is a Wharton Student Experience Fund Fellow. Before Wharton he spent six years in Canadian energy: three years as a Lead Project Engineer at Kiewit on the Trans Mountain Expansion pipeline (2020 to 2023), then three years at Enbridge in the New Energy Technologies group in Calgary (2023 to 2026), first as an engineer and then as AI Integration Lead, working on hydrogen, carbon capture and storage, and renewable natural gas.

His professional focus is the gap between design and operation. Many technically sound energy projects stall because risk tolerance, capital allocation and stakeholder alignment fail, not because the engineering does. He works where engineering, operations, risk and organizational readiness meet.

## What he did in energy

- Kiewit: lead field engineer on the Trans Mountain Expansion project, coordinating 36 field personnel and 22 subcontractors on the project's most complex construction spreads. Built costing scripts that cut subcontractor costs 18%. Kiewit Spotlight Award; selected as a 2022 Engineering Conference speaker.
- Operational readiness for new asset classes: launched Enbridge's CO2 operational readiness program, directing about 20 workstream managers to build the company's first CO2 operating model and ready two first-of-a-kind carbon assets for service, without formal authority over any of the workstreams.
- Investment analysis: owned investment cases for seven low-carbon opportunities, screened from more than 60 technologies, and led diligence on a CO2 storage joint venture. Moved conceptual engineering and early economics in-house, reducing reliance on external consultants.
- AI and analytics tooling: built a Monte Carlo fleet-reliability simulator in eight weeks that informed a multimillion-dollar equipment-sparing decision and is now used for ongoing fleet-capacity planning. Founded Enbridge's first cross-business AI community and helped 24 colleagues onboard the internal AI platform.
- External knowledge capture: represented the company at clean-energy conferences on hydrogen, carbon management and alternative fuels, and turned what he heard into internal guidance. Known for separating technology maturity from hype.

## Leadership outside work

- Young Professionals in Energy (Calgary): joined as a volunteer, chaired the flagship Future Leaders Dinner (120+ attendees, sold out), helped a divided board align on strategy, and was elected 2026 chapter President. The chapter delivered its strongest programming year.
- Interstitial Energy and Avatar Innovations: CEO and co-founder of Interstitial Energy, a hydrogen-storage startup, which he led through Avatar Innovations' nine-month energy venture studio. Negotiated a Sandia National Laboratories partnership, secured backing from Avatar, CRIN and Fluor, and won the program's GRIT Award for execution after a major pivot. The experience convinced him that clean-energy scaling problems are execution and capital problems as much as technology problems.
