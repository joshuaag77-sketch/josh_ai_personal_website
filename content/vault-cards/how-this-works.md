title: "How this vault works"
---

This chatbot is the public interface to Josh's second brain, a personal knowledge system he built in Obsidian. The real vault lives on his machine: about three hundred linked notes, including one note per person in his network, synthesized from years of mentor conversations, career assessments, articles, books, and his own builds. He captures everything as plain markdown files he owns forever, then uses AI to read across all of it and find patterns no single note shows. A live export of the graph's structure is explorable in 3D at /brain on this site.

What the chatbot can see is a deliberately curated public slice, cards Josh wrote and approved for exactly this purpose, plus his published articles and the note titles of the public graph. His private reflections, journal, relationships, faith life, work specifics, and the names of people in his network stay on his machine. That is by design.

Why it exists: Josh believes the hard part of AI is connecting models to real context, and this site is his working example. The same approach (capture everything, connect it, let AI read across it) is what he builds professionally.
